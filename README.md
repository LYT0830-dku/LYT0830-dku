# Lunyu Zhao

I'm an undergraduate researcher at Duke Kunshan University and a member of the
[DKU Edge Intelligence Lab](https://github.com/Edge-Intelligence-Lab). I work
on resource-efficient and trustworthy LLM systems, with a focus on on-device
fine-tuning, federated learning, preference optimization, and AI safety.

## Selected Research

- **[MobileFineTuner](https://github.com/Edge-Intelligence-Lab/MobileFineTuner)**
  ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08211)) — a mobile-native C++ framework
  for on-device LLM fine-tuning on commodity smartphones. Conditionally
  accepted at ACM/IEEE SenSys 2027; co-first author.
- **[EdgeFlowerTune](https://github.com/AndyLu666/EdgeFlowerTune)**
  ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08636)) — a real-device benchmark and
  evaluation framework for federated LLM fine-tuning under heterogeneous
  edge-system constraints. Manuscript under review.
- **[MobileRLHF](https://github.com/Edge-Intelligence-Lab/MobileRLHF)** — a
  federated preference-learning system enabling phone-side KTO/LoRA adaptation
  from private user feedback. Accepted to the ACM MobiCom 2026 Work-in-Progress
  track.
- **FedHealthAgent** — a federated on-device health agent combining
  population-level guidance, exact local wearable evidence, and preference
  post-training. Submitted to ACM MobiCom 2027; under review.
- **Federated LLM Safety** — independent research on how federated LoRA
  aggregation changes safety behavior and task retention; public research
  artifacts are in preparation.

## Research Interests

On-device LLMs · Federated LLMs · ML Systems · Trustworthy LLMs & AI Safety
