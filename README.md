# LYT0830-dku

I work on resource-efficient and trustworthy language-model systems, with a
focus on on-device learning, federated LLM fine-tuning, preference
optimization, and safety under heterogeneous edge constraints. I am a member
of the [DKU Edge Intelligence Lab](https://github.com/Edge-Intelligence-Lab).

## Selected research

- **[MobileFineTuner](https://github.com/Edge-Intelligence-Lab/MobileFineTuner)**
  — a native C++ framework for fine-tuning LLMs on mobile devices.
- **[MobileRLHF](https://github.com/Edge-Intelligence-Lab/MobileRLHF)** —
  real-device federated preference post-training with LoRA, DPO, and KTO.
- **[EdgeFlowerTune](https://github.com/AndyLu666/EdgeFlowerTune)** — a
  deployment-oriented benchmark for federated LLM fine-tuning across
  heterogeneous edge devices.
- **FedHealthAgent** — a privacy-preserving mobile health research system with
  federated and on-device LLM integrations.
- **Federated LLM Safety** — independent research on the safety and robustness
  of federated LLM fine-tuning; public artifacts are in preparation.

## Research interests

On-device ML · Federated learning · LLM systems · Trustworthy ML
